Research & learning

Curious about what makes AI systems reliable.

My research interest is applied: how to move from a clever demo to a system that is grounded, testable and useful.

Agent architecture

Planner vs router patterns, tool selection, structured outputs, failure recovery and when a workflow should be deterministic rather than autonomous.

Retrieval quality

Chunking, embeddings, vector search, multimodal retrieval, reranking and grounding as levers for reducing irrelevant context and hallucination.

Evaluation & observability

Quality datasets, traceability, failure analysis, latency/cost awareness and evaluation loops that help teams improve AI applications over time.

Research direction informed by current 2026 AI engineering role requirements and portfolio guidance: proof of work, measurable retrieval/agent behavior, failure handling and production-minded engineering are stronger signals than a long tool list.
